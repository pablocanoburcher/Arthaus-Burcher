Return Policy

At Arthaus, we want you to be completely in love with your new art. If for any reason you are not satisfied, we accept returns within 30 days of delivery. To be eligible for a return, your item must be unused and in the same condition that you received it. It must also be in the original packaging.

Damaged Artwork

In the unfortunate event that your order arrives damaged, please email us immediately at pcanoburcher@gmail.com with photos of the damage and the packaging. We will send out a replacement free of charge as soon as possible.

Custom Orders

Due to the bespoke nature of personalized prints, these items are non-refundable unless they arrive damaged or defective. We provide digital proofs for all custom orders to ensure you are happy with the design before printing.

Refund Process

Once your return is received and inspected, we will send you an email to notify you that we have received your returned item. If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your credit card or original method of payment within 10 business days.

Questions?

If you have any questions about our return policy, please don't hesitate to contact us.
